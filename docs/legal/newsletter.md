## Consent

- You only join by typing your email into the sign-up box yourself.
- I send one confirmation email; nothing else arrives until you click its link.
  An address that isn't confirmed within 7 days is deleted.
- Signing up for the newsletter is separate from everything else: grading your portfolio or signing in never adds you to it.

## Unsubscribe

- Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom, and supports one-click unsubscribe in mail apps that offer it.
- One click is enough: no login, no "are you sure", no survey.
- Unsubscribes take effect right away, and at most within 10 business days as the law requires.
- After you unsubscribe, your address stays only on a do-not-email list, so it's never emailed again.

## Your address

- Your email is used only to send this newsletter.
- It's never sold, rented or shared, except with the service that sends the email (Cloudflare Email Service).
- More in the [privacy page](privacy.md).

## In every email

Each email carries:

- who it's from and an honest subject line;
- the unsubscribe link;
- no postal address for now: the newsletter is editorial and PortfolioRank sells nothing, so it is not commercial email. If PortfolioRank ever adds ads, sponsors or paid features, a PO box or virtual mailbox goes in the footer (owner decision, 2026-11-10).

## Limits

PortfolioRank sends all its email through one gate with a hard daily cap.
If the cap is hit, the rest of that week's emails wait for the next day rather than going out unchecked.

## Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters to you, the next email says so.
