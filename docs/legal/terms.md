## What the site is

PortfolioRank ranks public developer portfolios from people's votes, an AI grade and automatic checks.
It's free, has no paid plan, and can change or stop at any time.

## Play fair

- Vote honestly: one person, one vote.
  Don't use several accounts, scripts or paid voters to move a site.
- Don't scrape the site in bulk, overload it, or get around its limits and bot checks.
- Only claim, hide or remove a portfolio that's yours.
- Don't put anything unlawful, hateful or someone else's private details in a list name or a claim note.

I can remove votes, lists, claims or accounts that break these rules, and block whoever made them.

## Checking a site

Only check or grade sites that are public.
Checking a site adds it to PortfolioRank; you can ask for removal at any time (see [removal.md](removal.md)).

## Grades are opinions

Grades, ranks and posts are opinions, from people's votes and an AI judge, measured against published criteria.
They are not a verdict on the developer, and they can be wrong.
If something is wrong, tell me and I'll fix it.
