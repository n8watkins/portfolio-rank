# Remove me, or don't name me

> **DRAFT, not legal advice.** Not linked from the site yet. See [README.md](README.md).

Last updated: [owner: date it goes live].

PortfolioRank lists real people's portfolios, ranks them, and writes about what it finds.
If you're one of them, you decide how much of that you want.
No reason needed, no questions asked.

## Three things you can ask for

1. **Remove me.**
   Your site leaves PortfolioRank: the grid, face-offs, the leaderboard, the blog and the newsletter.
   It is never added back, even if it's still on the community list.
2. **Don't name me.**
   Your site stays listed and ranked, but posts, the newsletter and social posts never name you, link you or show your screenshot.
   You still show up in totals ("1 of 2,103 sites").
3. **Fix my listing.**
   A new address, a new name, or a fresh screenshot and grade after a redesign.

## How to ask

- **Public:** open a "Remove my portfolio" issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/n8watkins/portfolio-rank/issues/new/choose).
  Issues are public, so anyone can see the request.
- **Private:** email nate@n8builds.dev with your site's address and which of the three you want.

I don't ask you to prove the site is yours for "remove me" or "don't name me": both only take something away, so there's nothing to abuse.
For "fix my listing", I check the request comes from the site's owner, for example from an email on the site's domain or the GitHub account the site links to.

## What happens, and when

- I act on requests within 14 days.
- **Remove me:** the site goes into PortfolioRank's exclusion list, so the next daily update drops it.
  Its screenshots and stored page data are deleted 30 days later.
  Past votes stay in the vote log, which the leaderboard is computed from, but your site is no longer shown anywhere.
  They're anonymous: a vote says which two sites were compared, never anything about the site's owner.
- **Don't name me:** your site is added to a do-not-name list that every post and newsletter is checked against before it goes out; a draft that names you is dropped.
- **Already published:** any post or newsletter archive page that names you is edited to remove your name, link and screenshot within the same 14 days.
  Emails that were already sent can't be recalled.
- The [removed page](https://portfoliorank.dev/removed) counts removal requests but never says whose they were.

## How people are written about anyway

Even without a request, PortfolioRank names individual developers only for good reasons: top sites, big climbers, redesigns that paid off.
Low scores and anything critical are only ever discussed in aggregate.
A post that breaks this rule is dropped automatically before it's published.
Details: [ai-content.md](ai-content.md).

## The community list

PortfolioRank's list comes from [emmabostian/developer-portfolios](https://github.com/emmabostian/developer-portfolios).
Removing yourself from PortfolioRank doesn't remove you from that list; to leave it too, open a pull request or issue there.
