TL;DR

  • Adding Projects screens (version B) changed no verdict: 0 changes in 40 comparisons (2 models, batched and one at a time, 10 sites). Every AI judge says 9 portfolios and 1 not (vallarasuk.com, the hijacked gambling site) in both versions.
  • B did not improve the project scores. On the 6 sites where B added screens, Gemini 3.1 moved +0.33 batched and -0.17 one at a time, and Haiku moved 0.00 batched; that is within the noise of the 4 sites where B added nothing (-0.25 to -0.50). Haiku one at a time went down by a full point on average (5 of 6 sites down).
  • The reason: the extra screens rarely showed projects. Only 3 of 10 pages have a Projects heading the cut could find, and one of those (shubhadeepsec.github.io) showed the intro overlay; 3 got the "second screen" fallback (an About section, stats, a gambling pop-up), which Haiku read as "no projects here"; 4 pages are one screen tall, so B added nothing.
  • Where B did show real project cards (luispeche.vercel.app), Gemini 3.1 batched raised projects from 4 to 5 and visual design from 4 to 5: the idea works when the cut lands on the projects.
  • B costs about 25% more input tokens for both models (Haiku $0.0292 against $0.0282 for 12 requests; Gemini free tier), and no extra time to speak of.
  • The code-only check (v2.1) calls vallarasuk.com a portfolio: a hijacked spam page is not an exclusion code can see, while both AI models catch it.
  • One capture gap: at f378754 the lean capture did not pass shubhadeepsec.github.io's "CLICK TO ENTER" intro, so its first screens show the overlay; the full-page lane fixed it at b6fa2d8 after this test was locked.
  • Recommendation for the 543: grade with version A (today's input) and v2.1; do not add Projects screens yet.

  • Run the 543 with version A: the two first screens plus the page text, and definition v2.1. Version B changes no verdict and does not reliably raise project scores, at about 25% more input tokens.
  • Capture the 543 with captureOnce at b6fa2d8 (not f378754), so intro screens like shubhadeepsec.github.io's are passed before the first screens are stored.
