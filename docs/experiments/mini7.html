Mini test 2: the new portfolio definition

13 November 2026 · prompt version "portfolio-definition-v2" (the locked prompts with only the is_portfolio section replaced by the owner's definition) · 5 new not-ranked sites, one per group (seed 20261114), plus agney.dev and abhisheksharma.codes from mini 5 · captured with the mini 5 rules (splash click-through, dialog default action, a scroll through the whole page, then the top screenshots) · two batches (the 5 new, then the 2 from mini 5), positions shuffled · graded by Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite (temperature 0.2, thinking "low") and Claude Haiku 5.5, plus a code-only check of the definition · job exp-mini7.

The code-only verdict applies the definition's three tests with no AI: a name (in the page title or top heading, or the listed name in the top of the page, spaced-out letters included), a role word in the top of the page, and a way to the work (a projects or work link in the first screen, a GitHub link, project links or a resume). It cannot see the exclusions (parked pages, placeholders, studio sites), so it only answers whether the three signs are there.

TL;DR

  • Under the new definition, Gemini 3.1 and Haiku agree on "real portfolio?" for all 7 sites: 6 yes, and no only for datasciencefolio.streamlit.app, which is an error screen.
  • The two regrades changed as intended: agney.dev and abhisheksharma.codes are now portfolios for both models (in mini 5, Haiku said no to both).
  • The code-only check agrees on 6 of 7. It says no to abhisheksharma.codes because no role word is on the page, which the definition requires; Haiku says yes anyway ("No role is stated, but no exclusion applies"). The definition's "false ONLY for" list wins over its three tests in the models' reading, so the two halves of the definition should be reconciled.
  • Scores are much closer than in mini 5: the same score on 13 of 21, one point apart on the other 8. Gemini 3.1 still gives more 5s (4 against 1).
  • Weak pages now get low scores instead of "not a portfolio": abhisheksharma.codes scores 1 for projects and writing, and pavithran26.net and shashikantportfolio.web.app score 1 or 2 for projects.
  • Graders see only the two first-screen screenshots plus the page text (up to 4,000 characters); the tall images are for this report.
  • heysai.dev opened on a dialog; the capture pressed Escape on desktop and phone. No other site needed a click.
  • Batch 2 holds only 2 sites, but the batch prompt was written for 5. Gemini 3.1 answered 5 sites twice (both rejected), so the batch wording now follows the number of sites. Haiku's batch 2 ran before that fix, with the five-site wording, and still answered the 2 sites correctly.
  • Cost: Gemini 3.1, 4 requests on the free tier (2 of them the rejected answers); Haiku, 2 requests for $0.0082.
